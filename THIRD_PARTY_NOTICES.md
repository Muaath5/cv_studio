## Latin Modern

Latin Modern fonts are by Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki, based on Donald E. Knuth's Computer Modern. They are distributed under the GUST Font License, an instance of the LaTeX Project Public License. See `licenses/latin-modern.txt` for the complete bundled copyright and license information.

The web fonts are WOFF conversions with the original internal font names preserved. The PDF assets are quadratic-outline derivatives, renamed internally as CV Studio / CVStudio. Their character maps and metrics are bundled alongside the font data. Original outlines and licensing remain attributable to the Latin Modern authors; these derivatives are distributed with the original license.
